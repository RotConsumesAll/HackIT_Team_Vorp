Exercises
└── Exer_XX
    ├── id 
    ├── pattern
    ├── english
    ├── semanticNote?
    ├── glossaryTerms
    ├── roles   → role name → required | expected | optional
    ├── answers → [ answer ]
    │              └── clauses → [ clause ]
    │                              └── segments → [ segment ]
    │                                                ├── role
    │                                                ├── forms [strings]
    │                                                └── optional? (boolean)
    └── hints   → order (string)
                  orderPairs (map of "a>>b" → string)
                  missing   (map of role → string)

pattern:
two-clause-sequence, 
yes-no-question+intent...



role names (part of speech)
    "subject"
    "object"
    "adverb"
    ...
Repeated allowed. Matching is positional.



answers: array of accepted variants
variants: a sentence
sentence: made up of clauses, ordered.
clauses: made up of segments
segments: POS, word variants, can omit?



glossaryTerms: manually defined local/slang terms
    "definition": "Market",
    "sampleSentence": "我要去巴刹买菜。"
    "standardEquivalent": "市场",



hints:
order: generic message
orderPairs: "subject>>frequency": "The frequency word sits after the subject."
missing: POS required (e.g. subject missing) or expected
